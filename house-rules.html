HOUSE RULES · RESPONSIBLE PLAY

Wind Table etiquette

Applies to The Wind Table gaming floor inside Tui & Fern, 22 Oriental Terrace, Wellington. Physical premises only.

01

Age and identity

You must be 20 years of age or older to enter The Wind Table. Present government photo identification at the host desk before play. Staff may refuse entry without explanation when ID is incomplete, expired, or unclear. Minors are not permitted in the gaming floor under any escort arrangement.

Hotel lodging for guests under 20 does not grant Wind Table access. Calm, kitchen, and nests remain available according to house policy.

02

Responsible gambling

Gambling at Tui & Fern is an on-site entertainment option inside a hotel — never a remote product. We do not offer online wagering, mobile betting apps, account deposits from home, or any form of off-premises play.

Before you sit:

  • Decide a spend limit and a time limit you can afford to lose.
  • Do not chase losses. Step away when the entertainment stops.
  • Take breaks. Food and water are available from the kitchen and brass lounge.
  • Do not play under the influence of alcohol beyond house service standards — staff may pause service.

Self-exclusion requests can be lodged at the host desk. We honour exclusions logged with the house and will refuse re-entry for the stated period.

03

Helpline and support

If gambling is causing harm to you or someone you know, contact the Gambling Helpline New Zealand:

  • Phone: 0800 654 655
  • Web: https://gamblinghelpline.co.nz

Help is confidential. Asking for support does not affect your right to lodge at Tui & Fern for non-gaming services.

04

Floor conduct

Photography of other patrons without consent is prohibited. Phones stay pocketed at tables unless staff approve a brief call away from the felt. Abusive language, intimidation, or interference with dealing ends the session.

Table limits, game availability, and opening hours are posted at the host desk and may change for private gatherings or maintenance. Chips remain property of the house until cashed under desk procedure.
