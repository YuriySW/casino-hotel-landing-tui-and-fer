Privacy notice

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Controller: Tui & Fern, 22 Oriental Terrace, Wellington 6011, New Zealand · hello@tuiandfern.com

01

Scope of this notice

This Privacy Notice explains how Tui & Fern (“we”, “us”, “the house”) collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you visit our website at tuiandfern.com, stay in our nests, dine in our kitchen, book calm treatments, hire gathering spaces, or enter The Wind Table gaming floor at our physical premises in Wellington. It applies to guests, walk-in patrons, event organisers, suppliers, and website visitors.

We operate a brick-and-mortar hotel with an on-premises gaming area. We do not operate online gambling platforms, remote betting services, or mobile wagering applications. Nothing in this notice creates such a service. Information collected for lodging, hospitality, and on-site play is handled under New Zealand privacy law, including the Privacy Act 2020.

02

What we collect

Depending on how you interact with the house, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details — name, email, phone, postal address, government photo ID details when required for Wind Table entry or check-in verification.
  • Stay and booking data — arrival and departure dates, nest type, preferences, dietary notes you volunteer, payment references processed through our payment providers.
  • Enquiry content — messages sent via the contact form on write.html or by email to hello@tuiandfern.com, including topic selections such as nests, kitchen, calm, gatherings, or Wind Table.
  • Visit and device data — approximate browser type, pages viewed, and essential preference flags stored locally for age confirmation and cookie acknowledgement (see our Cookie Notice).
  • Floor ledger notes — limited records of Wind Table entry checks, self-exclusion requests, and incident notes required for safety and legal compliance.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 20 for gaming purposes. Hotel stays involving minors are linked to a responsible adult’s booking record.

03

How we collect it

Information reaches us when you submit forms, call +64 4 801 4470, email the desk, present ID at reception or the Wind Table host desk, complete paper or digital registration at the property, or browse pages that set essential local storage flags. We may also receive details from travel agents or corporate bookers acting on your instruction.

Closed-circuit cameras may operate in public areas of the physical hotel for security. Footage is used for safety, incident review, and legal obligations — not for marketing analytics.

04

Why we use information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide and manage lodging, dining, wellness, and event services you request.
  • Verify age and identity for lawful access to The Wind Table and to enforce house rules and self-exclusion.
  • Respond to enquiries, complaints, and operational messages.
  • Process payments, issue invoices, and prevent fraud.
  • Maintain safety on the premises and meet regulatory, taxation, and licensing obligations applicable to a Wellington hotel with an on-site gaming floor.
  • Improve website reliability and keep essential preference settings working.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use guest data to open remote gambling accounts because we do not offer remote gambling.

05

Legal bases and legitimate interests

Where the Privacy Act framework applies, we process information because it is necessary to perform a contract with you (for example, a nest reservation), because you have provided information for a requested service, because we have a legitimate interest in operating a safe hotel and complying with gaming-floor age controls, or because a law requires retention (for example, financial records).

When we rely on consent — such as optional marketing emails if we offer them in future — you may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing. Operational emails about a booking are not marketing.

06

Sharing and processors

We share information only as needed with: payment processors; IT and hosting providers that support tuiandfern.com and property systems; professional advisers; event partners you ask us to involve; and authorities when required by law or to protect rights, safety, or property. Wind Table compliance may require disclosure to regulators or agencies under applicable New Zealand gambling and hospitality rules.

International transfers, if any (for example cloud hosting), are limited to providers under contracts that require appropriate safeguards. Ask us for current processor categories if you need detail for a specific request.

07

Retention

Booking and invoice records are kept for the period required by tax and commercial law, typically up to seven years. Enquiry emails are retained while useful for follow-up, then deleted or archived. Wind Table entry and exclusion logs are kept for the period needed to honour exclusions and demonstrate compliance. CCTV is overwritten on a rolling schedule unless retained for an incident. Local storage age and cookie flags remain on your device until you clear them.

08

Security

We apply administrative and technical measures appropriate to a boutique hotel: access controls on desk systems, staff training on ID handling, and TLS for website forms. No method of transmission is perfectly secure; if you suspect misuse of your information, contact hello@tuiandfern.com immediately.

09

Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you may request access to personal information we hold about you, seek correction of inaccurate data, and raise concerns about our handling. To exercise rights, email hello@tuiandfern.com with enough detail for us to locate your record. We may need to verify identity before responding. You may also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz) if you are not satisfied with our response.

10

Children, cookies, and changes

Our website is not directed at children. Gaming areas are restricted to adults 20+. For cookies and local storage, read the Cookie Notice. We may update this Privacy Notice to reflect operational or legal changes; the “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material updates may be signalled on the website home page or by notice at reception.

Contact for privacy matters: Tui & Fern, 22 Oriental Terrace, Wellington 6011 · +64 4 801 4470 · hello@tuiandfern.com. Related documents: Terms of Use, House Rules.
