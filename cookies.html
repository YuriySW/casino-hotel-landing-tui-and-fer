Cookie notice

Last updated: 5 October 2026 · Tui & Fern, 22 Oriental Terrace, Wellington 6011 · hello@tuiandfern.com

01

What this notice covers

This Cookie Notice describes how the website tuiandfern.com uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies. It sits alongside our Privacy Notice and Terms of Use. The site describes a physical hotel in Wellington with an on-site gaming floor known as The Wind Table. It is not an online gambling product, and these technologies are not used to enable remote wagering.

When we say “cookies”, we also mean browser localStorage keys used by our age-check and preference scripts, because they perform a similar function: remembering a choice you made on this device.

02

Why we use them

We use storage technologies to:

  • Remember that you confirmed you are 20 years of age or older, so the age gate does not reappear on every page load during the same browser profile.
  • Remember that you acknowledged the cookie banner, so we do not show it repeatedly after acceptance.
  • Support basic site function, security headers delivered by the host, and load balancing or session continuity that our hosting environment may require.

We currently prioritise essential and functional storage. We do not operate a behavioural advertising network on this site. If we introduce analytics or marketing cookies later, we will update this notice and, where required, request additional consent.

03

Ledger of technologies

tf_age_ok (localStorage). Set when you select “Yes, I am 20+” on the age gate. Value is a simple confirmation flag. Purpose: age-gate suppression for adults viewing content that describes our physical gaming floor. Duration: until you clear site data. Script: static/js/age-check.js.

tf_cookie_ok (localStorage). Set when you accept the cookie banner. Purpose: banner suppression. Duration: until you clear site data. Script: static/js/age-check.js.

Host and security cookies. Our hosting, CDN, or TLS configuration may set strictly necessary cookies to route traffic, mitigate abuse, or maintain a secure connection. Names vary by provider and are limited to operational need.

Third-party embeds. The Find Us page may load a map embed from Google Maps. That third party may set its own cookies subject to Google’s policies. If you prefer not to load map cookies, do not open the map iframe or use browser controls that block third-party cookies.

Font and icon CDNs. We load Bebas Neue and Libre Baskerville via Google Fonts and Bootstrap Icons via jsDelivr. Those providers may receive technical request data (IP address, user agent) when your browser fetches assets. We do not control their independent cookies, if any.

04

Legal basis and age gate

Essential storage for site security and the age gate is used because it is necessary to deliver the website safely and to keep underage visitors from browsing pages that describe gaming facilities. Cookie banner acknowledgement is stored after you click Accept. Declining the age gate redirects you to access-denied.html and does not set tf_age_ok.

Confirming age on the website is not a substitute for presenting government photo ID at The Wind Table host desk on site. Website storage never authorises play.

05

How to control storage

You can clear cookies and localStorage through your browser settings. After clearing, the age gate and cookie banner will appear again. You may also use private browsing modes, which typically discard storage when the session ends. Blocking all storage may prevent the age gate from remembering your confirmation and can cause repeated prompts.

Most browsers allow you to block third-party cookies while allowing first-party essential storage. Refer to your browser help documentation for steps specific to Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge, or other clients.

06

Do Not Track and signals

There is no uniform industry response to Do Not Track browser signals. We do not currently alter essential age or cookie acknowledgement behaviour based on DNT headers. If we add optional analytics in future, we will describe how preference signals are honoured.

07

What we do not use storage for

We do not use cookies or localStorage to place wagers, open remote gambling accounts, synchronise chip balances across devices, or track play away from Oriental Terrace. The Wind Table exists only as a physical room inside the hotel. Website storage cannot authorise entry, cash chips, or override a host-desk refusal.

We also do not sell cookie profiles to data brokers. Essential flags stay on your device for house function: age confirmation and banner acknowledgement. If a future feature requires optional analytics, we will label it clearly in this ledger and update the banner copy so you can decide before non-essential tags load.

08

Retention on your device versus our servers

Age and cookie acknowledgement values live in your browser’s localStorage. Clearing site data removes them immediately on that profile. Separately, if you submit the contact form, the message content travels to our desk systems under the Privacy Notice retention rules — that is not a cookie; it is a deliberate enquiry you typed.

Server logs from hosting may record IP addresses and request paths for security and uptime monitoring. Those logs are operational records, rotated on the host’s schedule, and are not combined with marketing segments because we do not run a marketing pixel stack on this build.
