01 / NAME

Tui & Fern

We took the name from two constants on this hill edge: the tui that cuts across Oriental Bay mornings, and the fern that holds the courtyard shade. The hotel is lodging first — keys, linen, kitchen, calm — with The Wind Table as an on-site evening for guests and patrons who pass the age desk.

HarbourLedger is our internal nickname for how we run the house: numbered stamps, dense directories, sharp edges. No soft pill navigation. No remote wagering products dressed as hospitality.

02 / BUILD

Steel, paper, teal

The facade reads industrial against the bay light. Inside, paper walls and harbour teal accents keep the ledger legible. Every floor connects on foot — that is the point of a physical hotel.
